# Reviews

Reviews are structured sessions for maintaining system health and staying aligned with what matters. Different reviews serve different purposes and cadences.

## Inbox Processing

**Cadence:** Anytime (daily recommended, or when inbox feels full)

Process items captured in the inbox one by one. For each item:

1. **Clarify** — What is it? Is it actionable?
2. **Decide** — If actionable: do it now (< 2 min), delegate, or defer. If not: trash, incubate, or file as reference.
3. **Organize** — Move to the appropriate place (task, project note, area reference, etc.)

The goal is **inbox zero** — not "done everything" but "decided on everything." Nothing lives in the inbox permanently.

## Weekly Review

**Cadence:** Weekly (GTD cornerstone practice)

The weekly review gets your head clear and your system current. Typical flow:

1. **Get Clear**
   - Process inbox to zero
   - Collect loose notes, papers, thoughts
   - Review previous week's calendar for open loops

2. **Get Current**
   - Review all active projects — is each one moving? What's the next action?
   - Review waiting-for items — any follow-ups needed?
   - Review upcoming calendar — anything to prepare for?

3. **Get Creative**
   - Review someday/maybe list
   - Consider new projects or ideas
   - Ask: "What's on my mind that I haven't captured?"

The weekly review is the "trust reset" — after completing it, you can trust your system reflects reality.

## Cleanup

**Cadence:** As needed (often combined with weekly review, or when things feel stale)

System maintenance focused on removing cruft and fixing broken things:

- **Stale tasks** — Items that are no longer relevant. Archive or delete.
- **Stuck projects** — Projects with no recent activity. Revive or move to someday/maybe.
- **Orphaned tasks** — Tasks not connected to any project or area. Reconnect or reconsider.
- **Broken references** — Links or associations that no longer work. Fix or remove.
- **Status drift** — Items marked "in progress" that aren't actually being worked on. Update status.

Think of cleanup as weeding a garden — removing what's dead so what's alive can thrive.

## Area Reflection

**Cadence:** Monthly (or quarterly)

A higher-altitude review examining your areas of responsibility as a whole:

1. **Review each area** — Is it getting appropriate attention? Neglected? Overweighted?
2. **Check alignment** — Do your active projects and tasks reflect what actually matters in this area?
3. **Surface gaps** — What's missing? What commitments have you made that aren't captured?
4. **Adjust balance** — Are some areas consuming disproportionate energy? Is that intentional?

Areas represent ongoing commitments (health, career, family, finances). Unlike projects, they don't end — area reflection ensures you're tending to all of them, not just the loudest ones.

This review connects daily actions to longer-term life priorities.
