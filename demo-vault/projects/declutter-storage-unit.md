## The Problem

Been paying $150/month for a storage unit that's mostly full of stuff I haven't touched in years. Time to deal with it.

## Approach

1. Schedule a full day to go through everything
2. Sort into: Keep, Sell, Donate, Trash
3. List sellable items on [[Facebook Marketplace]]
4. Donate anything usable to [[Local Tech Charity]]
5. Either downsize to smaller unit or eliminate completely

## What's In There

From memory:
- Old furniture from previous apartment
- Boxes of books
- Some camping gear
- Old electronics (probably e-waste at this point)
- Seasonal decorations
- Mystery boxes (who knows)

## Why Now

The monthly cost adds up. $150 × 12 = $1,800/year for stuff I don't use. That money could fund actual experiences or investments.

## When to Do This

Need a free weekend with good weather (parking lot work). Spring would be ideal.
