## Tasks

Tasks are actionable items. They should be small and concrete.

```yaml title="tasks/fix-login-bug.md"
---
title: Fix login bug
status: in-progress
created-at: 2025-01-03
updated-at: 2025-01-03
due: 2025-01-05
projects:
  - "[[Q1 Planning]]"
---

The SSO flow fails for enterprise users...
```

## Projects

Projects are collections of tasks with a clear end goal. They're "finishable."

## Areas

Areas are ongoing responsibilities that never end — like "Health" or "Work."
