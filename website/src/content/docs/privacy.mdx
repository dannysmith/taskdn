## Summary

Taskdn stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit your tasks, projects, or areas.

## What We Collect

### Website Analytics

This website uses [Simple Analytics](https://simpleanalytics.com/) to understand how people use the documentation. Simple Analytics:

- Does not use cookies
- Does not track individuals
- Is GDPR compliant
- Collects only aggregate page view data

### Desktop App

The desktop app is entirely offline. It:

- Stores all data in local files you control
- Does not phone home
- Does not collect usage analytics
- Does not require an account

### CLI

The CLI operates entirely on local files and does not make network requests.

## Your Data

All Taskdn products store data as markdown files on your local filesystem. You own your data completely. There are no cloud services, no syncing, no accounts.
