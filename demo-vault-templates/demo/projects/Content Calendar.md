## Overview

Create a content calendar for the quarter to maintain consistent visibility and build audience.

## Channels

1. **Blog** - Long-form content (2x per month)
2. **Twitter/X** - Daily engagement, weekly threads
3. **LinkedIn** - Professional updates (weekly)
4. **Newsletter** - Monthly roundup

## Themes

- Month 1: Goals and fresh starts
- Month 2: Workflow and productivity
- Month 3: Learning and skill development

## Content Ideas

### Month 1
- [ ] Goals and focus areas for the quarter
- [ ] Tool/stack updates

### Month 2
- [ ] How I structure my work week
- [ ] Favorite productivity tools

### Month 3
- [ ] Learning in public retrospective
- [ ] Quarter wrap-up and a preview of the next one

## Tracking

Use simple spreadsheet to track:
- Planned vs published
- Engagement metrics
- What's working
